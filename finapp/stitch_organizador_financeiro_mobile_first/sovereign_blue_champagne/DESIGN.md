## Brand & Style

This design system expresses private banking prestige, fiduciary integrity, and bespoke wealth stewardship. It serves high-net-worth individuals, institutional wealth managers, and discerning users who demand absolute clarity and dignified restraint from financial tooling.

The visual style unites **Warm Modern Minimalism** with **Tailored Editorial Polish**:
- Architectural balance with generous negative space to minimize cognitive load during complex financial management.
- Noble champagne accents used exclusively to draw focus toward high-value metrics, portfolio appreciation, and sovereign actions.
- Tactile, porcelain-like card surfaces set against delicate pearlescent canvas planes, discarding abrasive high-contrast borders in favor of soft, diffused tonal containment.
- Calm, unwavering stability conveying fiscal precision, durability, and discreet luxury.

## Colors

The palette establishes an atmosphere of architectural permanence and fiscal authority:

- **Primary (`#1E3A8A`)**: Deep Royal Navy. Anchors navigation headers, essential typography, prominent buttons, and structural boundaries. Conveys institution-grade security and permanence.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`)**: Champagne Ochre / Refined Gold. Reserved for deliberate focal points: investment yields, active selection states, prestige markers, and subtle key performance highlights. It is never applied across massive text blocks or loud banners.
- **Tertiary (`#1D4ED8`)**: Vibrant Royal Blue. Powers interactive states, live transaction badges, links, and active progress indications.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FDFBF7`)**: Warm Pearlescent Off-White. Softens the display contrast compared to sterile digital white, reducing eye fatigue and establishing a tailored parchment sensation.
- **Surface Neutrals**:
  - `surface-base`: `#FDFBF7` (Application foundation)
  - `surface-raised`: `#FFFFFF` (Elevated cards and modal surfaces)
  - `surface-subtle`: `#F1F5F9` (Data tables, input backgrounds, structural containers)
  - `text-primary`: `#0F172A` (Slate obsidian for primary reading comfort)
  - `text-secondary`: `#475569` (Muted financial ledger labels and metadata)
  - `border-hairline`: `rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.08)` (Subtle chromatic containment)

## Typography

Typography relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its balanced geometry, sculpted terminals, and legible proportions at data-dense scales.

- **Tabular Figures (`tnum`)**: Enable OpenType tabular lining figures globally for all financial balances, portfolio metrics, transaction logs, and timestamps to preserve horizontal vertical alignment.
- **Hierarchy & Restraint**: Maintain high visual contrast between headers and support labels. Section titles use medium and semi-bold weights rather than heavy black styles to retain elegance.
- **Caps Styling**: Apply `label-sm` with subtle letter spacing strictly to micro-headings, data column titles, and asset identifiers (e.g., `USD`, `EUR`, `IBAN`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on a rigid 8px baseline rhythm across an adaptive fluid grid:

- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a max canvas width of 1280px centered with 40px (`2.5rem`) outer canvas margins. Multi-column financial tables, portfolio allocation splits, and analytic modules sit side by side.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px margins. Sidebar controls collapse into horizontal headers or tab navigation.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px)**: 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) side margins. Complex asset distributions collapse into progressive disclosure cards and vertical stacks.
- **Rhythm Discipline**: Component interiors maintain strict symmetric padding: `space-md` (16px) for compact table cells and inputs; `space-lg` (24px) for cards, balance overviews, and executive dashboards.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids harsh drop-shadows or stark borders, opting instead for **Tonal Stratification and Tinted Ambient Diffusion**:

- **Subtle Chromatic Shadows**: Shadows use subtle Royal Blue tints rather than flat gray or pure black, creating a sense of volume that feels integrated into the light environment:
  - *Resting Card Elevation*: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.03)`
  - *Floating Layer / Flyout Elevation*: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.04)`
  - *Modal Elevation*: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`
- **Boundary Precision**: All elevated containers combine ambient diffusion with a fractional hairline perimeter: `border: 1px solid rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.06)`. This ensures distinction over the `#FDFBF7` canvas without harshness.
- **Layer Stacking**:
  - `0`: Pearl canvas foundation (`#FDFBF7`)
  - `1`: Recessed data reservoirs (`#F1F5F9`)
  - `2`: Primary porcelain action cards (`#FFFFFF`)
  - `3`: Dropdown overlays, active transaction sheets, and tooltips

## Shapes

The interface embraces a balanced curvature model (`roundedness: 2`):

- **Component Standard (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Standard buttons, input text boxes, asset chips, segmented selectors, and dropdown menus.
- **Surfaces & Cards (`rounded-lg: 1rem` / 16px)**: Primary portfolio cards, account summary containers, chart modules, and transaction groups.
- **Modals & Overlays (`rounded-xl: 1.5rem` / 24px)**: Wire transfer modals, signature sheets, and full account statement viewports.
- **Pills**: Badges and status pills use a fully rounded border radius for maximum contrast against rectilinear financial charts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Royal Navy (`#1E3A8A`) background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.5rem` radius, 12px vertical by 20px horizontal padding. On hover: shifts to Tertiary Royal (`#1D4ED8`) with gentle translation.
- **Secondary / Champagne Accent**: Subtle warm champagne tint background (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.12)`), text color in deep champagne (`#9A7932`), border `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)`. Used for wealth advisory actions and premier features.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent background, text in `#1E3A8A`. Hover brings a soft slate tint (`#F1F5F9`).

### Input Fields
- Built on a `#FFFFFF` base with a hairline border `1px solid rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.15)`.
- Resting height of 44px with 14px horizontal inset padding.
- Focused state: border transitions to `#1D4ED8` accompanied by an ambient glow ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.12)`.
- Helper text in `text-secondary` (`#475569`); monetary suffixes (`USD`, `BRL`, `EUR`) fixed right in `label-sm` with subtle champagne tint.

### Cards & Surfaces
- High-grade white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with `1rem` radius and 24px inner padding.
- Header area separates metrics from card content via hairline rule or 16px bottom margin.
- Optional champagne accent: a 2px top border accent in `#C5A059` for highlighted accounts (e.g., Private Wealth or High-Yield Vaults).

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with 4px vertical, 10px horizontal padding.
- Neutral Chip: `#F1F5F9` background with `#1E3A8A` text.
- Yield / Positive Return Chip: Pale sage background (`#ECFDF5`), forest text (`#065F46`).
- Premium / Status Chip: Soft gold tone (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.14)`), text in `#8C6D23`.

### Lists & Financial Ledgers
- Rows utilize alternating hover transitions using `#F8FAFC`.
- Cells maintain strict `space-md` vertical padding with tabular figures on monetary columns.
- Hairline divider (`1px solid rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.05)`) between items, dropping the final divider.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- Checkbox: 18px square with `0.25rem` corners; active check uses `#1E3A8A` background with crisp white tick.
- Segmented Control (Timeframes: 1D, 1W, 1M, 1Y, ALL): Enclosed in `#F1F5F9` track with 4px internal padding; active item transitions to `#FFFFFF` with soft shadow and `#1E3A8A` bold text.

### Account Wealth Card (Custom Specialized Component)
- Distinctive hero card designed for liquid balance overview: incorporates a subtle radial ambient gradient background from `#1E3A8A` to `#172554`, crisp white text, and `#E5C158` champagne accents for yield percentages and tier insignias.
